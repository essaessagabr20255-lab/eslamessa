About Me

Turning Ideas Into Digital Reality

Engineer by craft, entrepreneur by mindset, mentor by passion.

I'm Eslam Essa Gabr — a software engineer and full-stack developer from Port Said, Egypt, and the Founder & CEO of Tamooh EdTech Platform. My work sits at the intersection of software engineering, educational technology, entrepreneurship and business development: I design and ship digital products, grow an EdTech venture, and help people build careers that actually last.

On the technical side, I deliver complete web and software solutions — from responsive frontends to robust backends, RESTful APIs and ERP systems — with more than 20 delivered projects and a 90% goal-achievement rate across ERP engagements. I hold a Software Engineering Diploma from ALX Africa and a Bachelor of Science in Specific Education from Port Said University, a combination that shapes how I build technology and how I teach it.

On the human side, I've dedicated my career to freelancing training and career development. As a trainer and mentor working with organizations such as ITI, NTI, DEPI, Career 180, Freelance Yard and Creativa Innovation Hub — and through entrepreneurship and incubation training with ITIDA & TIEC — I've guided more than 3,000 students and trainees and celebrated 450+ freelancing success stories.
